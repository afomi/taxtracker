# Life experiences

A tax is levied in a moment, for a person, in a transaction.

[Performance.gov](https://www.performance.gov/cx/projects/) calls these moments life experiences.
A birth, a financial shock, recovering from a disaster, and leaving military service are the federal examples.
The organizing unit is the person's situation.
The agencies show up inside it.

[qart-ux](https://github.com/afomi/qart-ux) and [rpms-ux](file:///Users/ryan/workspace/rpms-ux) (`~/workspace/rpms-ux`) draw the same idea as a journey.
Each step is a person doing one thing, and the step names the screen that thing lands on.
Those two sites keep the steps in BPMN.
TaxTracker can name the person and the moment first.
The diagram can wait.

## The moment is the picture

Sales tax is a person buying from a vendor.
Milk, coffee, and food are the first scene.
Every sales and use tax that reaches that counter is a layer on that one purchase.

Property tax is a property transaction.
The bill, the transfer, and the assessments meet the same parcel.
The picture is the closing, or the bill on the table.

A business license is the certificate, and the errand that produces it.

The types page does not yet list seventy types.
The inventory holds a few dozen records of a handful of types.
When the long list exists, the drawings stay few.
Many taxes share one moment.

Drawings follow qart's ink and wash (`~/workspace/qart/design/ink-and-wash/README.md`).
Loose black ink, two pigments, white paper, objects only.
No people, no faces, no letters, no numbers.
A rate does not appear in the drawing.
A row marked sketch is a moment we have not tied to a sourced record.
