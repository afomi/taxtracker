Circular flows prototype

A force-directed view of economic circulation: households earn wages from both government and businesses, pay taxes, and government spending returns to households and businesses. Sinks (savings, imports, debt service) drain from the cycle.

Taxes (H→G) Taxes (B→G) Public wages (G→H) Contracts (G→B) Private wages (B→H) Sinks

Capital sinks & flows

  • Sinks: Savings/investment, imports/trade deficit, debt service (interest to external holders)
  • Sources: New money creation (Fed), exports, borrowing
  • Drag nodes to explore the structure. The circular layout emphasizes how wages and spending create feedback loops.
